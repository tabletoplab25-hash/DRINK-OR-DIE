Drink or Die

A game of potions, bluff & alchemy

🔥🧊✨💀🌿

You vs Morgana, Merlin & Circe — 90 cards

How to play: Create potions (1 face-down + 1 face-up). Players take turns adding a card or drinking. Whoever drinks takes all cards, then creates the next potion.
6-card limit: If the cauldron reaches 6 cards, the next player MUST drink!
Balance: Face-down and face-up counts must stay within 1 of each other.
Round ends when everyone has drunk. Refill to 6 cards.
Scoring: Raw = 🔥+🧊+✨+💀 minus 🌿. Then penalty = |imbalance − 🌿| × 3. Nature costs 1 pt but reduces imbalance that costs 3 per point!
