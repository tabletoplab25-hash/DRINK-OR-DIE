Drink or Die

A game of potions, bluff & alchemy

🔥🧊✨💀🌿

You vs Morgana, Merlin & Circe — 90 cards

How to play: Create potions (1 face-down + 1 face-up). All players take turns adding a card or drinking. Whoever drinks takes all cards, then creates the next potion. Drunk players still add cards!
6-card limit: If the cauldron reaches 6, next non-drunk player must drink.
Scoring: Raw = 🔥+🧊+✨+💀 minus 🌿. Penalty = |imbalance − 🌿| × 3. Nature costs 1 pt but saves 3 per point of imbalance!
